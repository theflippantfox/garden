# Welcome

This is a [[digital garden]] — a living collection of notes that connect to each other.
Unlike a blog, notes here are **evergreen**: they grow, get rearranged, and link to
each other.

## What to do here

- [[getting-started]] walks through the basics
- Browse by [[tag|tags]] to find what interests you
- Try the search box — it uses Fuse.js over a server-built index
